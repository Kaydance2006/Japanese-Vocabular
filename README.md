# Kotoba Quest (ことばクエスト)

A beginner Japanese vocabulary game for kids (about age 10). It practices **reading hiragana and katakana words**: nouns, verbs and a few describing words. The words come from the GENKI textbook pages (lessons 1–5) plus extra elementary words.

**To play:** open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge or Safari. Nothing needs to be installed. It works on a computer, tablet or phone.

## Games

| Game | What the child does |
|------|---------------------|
| えあわせ Picture Match | Reads a word and taps the matching picture. |
| ことばさがし Word Finder | Sees a picture and taps the matching word. |
| かるた Karuta | Hears a word and grabs the card with that word written on it. |
| もじならべ Word Builder | Puts kana tiles in order to spell the word, with look-alike decoy tiles (さ/ち, シ/ツ, ソ/ン …). |
| あなうめ Missing Kana | Finds the one kana missing from a word. |
| まるばつ Yes or No | Decides if a word matches a picture (⭕ / ❌). |
| ふうせんわり Balloon Pop | Pops the balloon with the right word before it floats away. |
| しんけいすいじゃく Memory Cards | Flips cards to match words with pictures. |

**Adventure** mixes the games in a different order every time. **Review** builds a game from the words the child has been missing.

## Reading help
- **Rōmaji switch** in the top bar turns rōmaji on or off at any time, even in the middle of a question. Rōmaji follows the GENKI style (sensee, otoosan, koohii).
- With rōmaji off, a **💡 Hint** button shows rōmaji for one question (that question then earns no star).
- English labels under pictures can be turned off in Settings.
- **Easy** gives 3 choices and shorter words; **Challenge** gives 4–6 choices with look-alike words and more decoy tiles.

## No repeated games
- Each game picks new words, spread across the word packs, and skips the words from the last game.
- Game sets are remembered, so the same set of questions is never dealt twice in a row (the last 40 are checked).
- Words use spaced practice: a missed word comes back soon, and a known word comes back after longer and longer gaps (4 hours, 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, about 2 weeks). This keeps old words fresh after the class has moved on to grammar.
- **My words** shows each word as new, learning, needs practice or mastered.

## Words (292)
Family, people & jobs, school & classroom, things, food & drinks, places, fun & hobbies, animals & nature, time & days (days of the week, clock times), numbers & ages (〜さい, 〜ふん), colors, countries, action words (verbs) and describing words. Extra words that aren't in the textbook pages (animals, fruit, colors, numbers and a few more) can be turned off with the **Extra words** setting. Word packs can be switched on and off in Settings.

Left out on purpose: おさけ (alcohol), and textbook words that can't be shown as a picture (これ, どこ, いつ …).

## Sound
- Words are read aloud by the device's built-in Japanese voice (Web Speech API). Every word has a 🔊 button, and Karuta has a 🐢 slower button.
- Sound effects are generated in the browser, so there are no audio files.
- If the device has no Japanese voice, a note on the home screen explains how to add one, and Karuta shows the picture so it can still be played.

## Rewards
- A red はなまる (the flower circle Japanese teachers draw on good work) for each right answer.
- ⭐ stars, streak bonuses, confetti, a day streak, and a new sticker for every 10 stars (40 stickers).

Progress is saved in the browser's local storage on that device.
